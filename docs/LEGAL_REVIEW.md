# Legal and Product Review Required

This register captures decisions the engineering audit cannot make. No legal wording has been drafted here. Items remain open until the responsible legal/privacy or product owner records an approved decision and implementation evidence.

| ID | Topic | Status | Decision / evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LEGAL-001 | LGPD legal bases and sensitive data | NEEDS-HUMAN | Identify the lawful basis for each purpose and data category. Specifically review medication/dosage/intake data, financial records, logbook entries, memories and inferences; decide whether a distinct, specific health consent is required and how it is recorded/revoked in the mobile/backend contract. |
| LEGAL-002 | Children’s data / age assurance | NEEDS-HUMAN | Decide whether the product is directed to minors, how self-reported age is handled, and the safeguards/basis for adult users entering identifiable childcare details about children under LGPD art. 14. A birth-date field is not independent age verification. |
| LEGAL-003 | Controller, encarregado and rights contact | NEEDS-HUMAN | Supply the legal controller identity and approved privacy/encarregado contact details for public pages and app-config. |
| LEGAL-004 | Purposes, processors and international transfer | NEEDS-HUMAN | Approve a purpose-by-purpose data inventory and complete processor/subprocessor list, including hosting, database, Redis, AI providers, Google Play and observability vendors. Review international-transfer mechanisms and contract clauses for US-hosted Render/AI processing. |
| LEGAL-005 | Retention schedule and deletion exceptions | NEEDS-HUMAN | Approve periods and legal holds for account data, conversations, AI usage, request/error/security logs, deletion requests, admin audit, billing events, exports and media. The current public AI-log retention claim is not backed by an inspected cleanup job; do not publish a replacement period until approved. |
| LEGAL-006 | ROPA and RIPD/DPIA | NEEDS-HUMAN | Determine and document whether a ROPA and RIPD/DPIA are required for health data, AI processing, children’s data and community reports. |
| LEGAL-007 | Data-subject rights operations | NEEDS-HUMAN | Approve identity verification and SLAs for access, correction, deletion, portability, consent withdrawal and deletion requests submitted without a password. Confirm treatment of backups, processors, Redis outages and files. |
| LEGAL-008 | Breach response (LGPD art. 48) | NEEDS-HUMAN | Name the incident owner, regulator/data-subject notification decision path, timelines, evidence retention and processor escalation contacts. No production incident-response integration was verified. |
| LEGAL-009 | ANVISA and medical scope | NEEDS-HUMAN | Obtain a qualified regulatory assessment of whether reminders, health logging or generated guidance create software-as-a-medical-device obligations. Confirm approved product boundaries and clinical safety review. |
| LEGAL-010 | Financial advice scope | NEEDS-HUMAN | Approve the boundary between record keeping/general education and regulated investment, credit, tax or financial recommendations; verify actual model output behavior. |
| LEGAL-011 | Consumer/subscription disclosures | NEEDS-HUMAN | Verify current price, billing interval, trial conversion, renewal, cancellation, refund and support disclosures against CDC/Decreto 7.962 and Google Play requirements in the actual store listing and purchase flow. Flutter source inspection is not Play Console verification. |
| LEGAL-012 | Google Play Data Safety and Health declarations | NEEDS-HUMAN | Reconcile Play Console Data Safety, Health apps declaration, account deletion URL, UGC moderation/reporting and AI-generated-content disclosures with actual production collection, transfers and deletion behavior. |
| LEGAL-013 | UGC and AI moderation | NEEDS-HUMAN | Define community/draft moderation rules, human escalation, appeal and response SLAs, retention of moderation evidence and reporting for generated content. Confirm the current flagging workflow is operationally staffed. |
| LEGAL-014 | Marco Civil and logging | NEEDS-HUMAN | Have counsel/operations classify retained access/security logs and determine applicable retention periods and access controls. The application no longer writes request IPs or validation values in the request logger, but platform/proxy logs were not inspected. |
| LEGAL-015 | Admin security and recovery | NEEDS-HUMAN | Decide mandatory production MFA, recovery method, operator access review and secret-rotation cadence; confirm the production enrollment path before enabling the setting. |
| LEGAL-016 | Backups and restore | NEEDS-HUMAN | Confirm database/media backup ownership, encryption, restore test cadence, recovery objectives, retention and deletion propagation into backup expiry. No production backup/restore run was possible in this workspace. |
